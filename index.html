About Me

I'm Sufal K.C., an Electronics Engineering student from Kathmandu who enjoys turning real problems into working hardware and software. I currently serve as Vice President of SEIS, and I've been Runner Up at the KathFest 3.0 Hackathon and won the Best Thematic Award at the Taranga Hardware Hackathon.

My work spans IoT systems built on ESP32 and Wi-Fi sensing, AI/ML projects like an eye-controlled wheelchair, and full-stack web apps. I care about building technology that is affordable, accessible and genuinely useful, and about growing the tech communities around me.
